02

The plan

⚠ Trial Reels — not normal Reels. Every single one.

A Trial Reel goes only to people who don't follow us. That's the entire point: this is for strangers, and our own followers aren't the audience. It also gives each run a fresh, clean sample — which is what makes them comparable to each other.

If one goes up as a normal Reel by mistake, that run is void. Don't count it, don't compare it.

Three creatives × three flows = nine combinations

Each Trial Reel is one creative paired with one DM flow. We work through every pairing, one every day or two, and see which combination lands.

The order matters for one small reason: every creative meets every flow exactly once. So at the end it reads two ways — average creative A's three runs to see how A did, average flow 1's three runs to see how flow 1 did. Same nine reels, two answers.

The rules while it runs

  • One live at a time. Post the next once the last has had at least 24 hours.
  • Roshan picks the video for every run. Free choice — different footage each time is better than the same clip nine times, and the platform prefers it too.
  • Log every run before the next goes up. Nine rows: run, creative, flow, date, views, likes, comments, DMs sent, emails captured, tutorial opened. No log, no test.
  • Kill anything under 0.3 comments per like. Note it and move on.
  • Anything over 1.0 is a winner — stop the rotation and run that combination properly.

05

The three ManyChat flows

All three trigger on the word CRIMP in a comment on that specific Trial Reel. All three also post a short public reply in the comment thread — that's another comment on the post, and it's free.

The follow ask — same in all three

⚠ Ask for the follow. Never block on it.

ManyChat can check whether she follows us. If she doesn't, put this line at the top of the first message and send the message anyway:

"Quick thing — follow @hollahair.pro so my messages reach you instead of landing in your requests folder 🤍"

Why it matters: @cocolifestyleresort's automation never replied to us, across two attempts, one of them as a follower. Their reel has 20,800 comments on it. Twenty thousand people raised their hand and nobody caught them. We don't repeat that.

Flow 1 · Email first

Copied from @shopfirsthealth, the best flow anyone in the set is running.

  1. Instantly
    Hi! 🤍 Thanks for commenting — I've got the full crimper prep tutorial for you. Want me to send it?
    Yes please
  2. When she taps the button
    Amazing. What's your email? That's where I'll send it, and you'll get the next tutorials before they go public.
  3. When she sends the email
    Sent 🤍 Here it is too, so you have it right now: [TUTORIAL LINK] Tell me how it goes on your next bride — I read every reply.
    Watch it now
  4. If she writes anything unexpected
    Let me get a human to come back to you on that 🤍
    Route it to a real person. Don't let the thread die.

Flow 2 · One message

Copied from @cooldownrunning, whose automation replied to us in under a minute.

  1. Instantly, and that's the whole flow
    Hey!! 🤍 Thanks so much for commenting. Here's the full crimper prep tutorial — the step that keeps an updo in through a whole wedding day: [TUTORIAL LINK] No catch, it's yours. If it helps, tell me and I'll send you the next one.
    No email asked. This is the run that tells us what the email ask is costing us.

Flow 3 · Give first, ask after

  1. Instantly
    Hi! 🤍 Here it is — the full crimper prep tutorial: [TUTORIAL LINK]
    Got it, thanks
  2. When she taps the button
    One more thing — what's your email? That's how I send the next tutorials before they go public.

What each flow is actually testing

1 vs 2 — does a conversation with a button and an email capture beat one straight message? 1 vs 3 — does asking for the email before or after handing over the tutorial matter? 2 vs 3 — is the button worth anything at all?

Every email captured goes to one dedicated Klaviyo list, so we can see which flow filled it fastest.

06

Before the first one goes up

Nothing new has to be built — and no new discount code

The give already exists: "Perfect Prep with a Crimper", the tutorial we already hand out in exchange for an email, with 10% off attached to it. There's already a Klaviyo flow behind it.

So no coupon to create, no program to launch, no page to write. The only new thing in this whole plan is the ManyChat automation.

The checklist

Later, not now

  • An affiliate landing page. There's a page at /pages/affilliate-registration that currently says only "share your favorite HOLLA tools and get a commission" — no rate, no terms, nothing that closes anyone. If we ever want to point this funnel at creators instead of at the tutorial, that page has to be written first. Not part of this plan.
  • The at-home customer version. Same nine-run structure, different give and different copy. Worth doing once we know which creative and which flow won here.
