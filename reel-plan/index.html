02

Our offer — the numbers, locked

Read first

This is identical in all nine runs. If the offer changes between runs we can't tell whether the reel won or the offer won — so it stays fixed, word for word.

What she getsThe number
Welcome discount on her first order20% off
Her own code, for friends and clientsthey get 10% off
Commission she earns on every order through her code10%
Bonus, every five orders — and it repeats$25
Posting requirednone

The line that does the work

Don't sell it as "10% plus a bonus" — nobody adds that up. Sell the total: "Every 5 friends, about $100 back."

The maths: 10% of five orders is around $75, plus the $25 bonus. $100 for five friends — and it repeats at ten, fifteen, twenty. That's the number that goes in the copy, because it's the one that sounds like real money.

And the part that keeps the reel honest

The reels promise that something gets sent to her, so something has to actually get sent. Twenty curling irons go out this month, drawn from the people who commented. That's what makes the hint true rather than a bait, and at cost it is the cheapest creative we will ever buy.

Which means the DM has two jobs, not one: confirm she's in the draw, and give her the offer meanwhile. Nobody is left thinking they were promised a gift and handed a coupon.

On content: we never require it. "No posting required" is the line that gets people in — it's the single best thing in @stasshsociety's DM and we're keeping it. But we do nudge: the girls who share a quick video get to five fastest. Encouragement, never a condition.

03

The plan — nine runs

⚠ Trial Reels — not normal Reels. Every single one.

A Trial Reel goes only to people who don't follow us. That's the entire point: this is for strangers, and our own followers aren't the audience. It also gives every run a fresh, clean sample, which is what makes them comparable.

If one goes up as a normal Reel by mistake, that run is void — don't count it and don't compare it.

Each Trial Reel is one creative paired with one DM flow. Every creative meets every flow exactly once, so at the end it reads two ways: average creative A's three runs to see how A did, average flow 1's three runs to see how flow 1 did. Same nine reels, two answers.

The rules while it runs

  • One live at a time. Post the next once the last has had at least 24 hours.
  • You pick the video for every run. Free choice, and different footage each time is better than the same clip nine times — the platform prefers it too. Curling iron, dryer, brushes: whatever looks best.
  • Log every run before the next goes up. Run, creative, flow, date, views, likes, comments, DMs sent, emails captured, sign-ups. No log, no test.
  • Kill anything under 0.3 comments per like. Note it and move on.
  • Anything over 1.0 is a winner — stop the rotation and run that combination properly.
04

The three reel creatives

The rule that makes or breaks this

The reel hints. The DM tells.

Look at what the brands actually put on screen: "We will send you a pr package", "looking for non-influencers to send PR to", "PR opportunity". Not one of them states a discount, a percentage or a commission. They promise that something will be sent to her, and they leave every detail out.

That vagueness is the whole engine. She fills in the blank herself, always with the best case, and a comment costs her one second. That is why twenty thousand people typed a word.

So nothing about 20% off, the code, or the $100 goes anywhere near the reel. All of it lives in the DM. A creative that answers her questions before she comments gives her no reason to comment.

You choose the footage. The on-screen text is what's being tested, so keep it exactly as written.

06

The three ManyChat flows

All three trigger on the word SALON in a comment on that specific Trial Reel. All three also post a short public reply in the comment thread — that's another comment on the post, and it's free. The offer inside them is identical. Only the structure differs, because that's what we're testing.

⚠ Ask for the follow. Never block on it.

ManyChat can check whether she follows us. If she doesn't, put this line at the top of the first message and send the message anyway:

"Quick thing — follow @hollahair.pro so my messages reach you instead of landing in your requests folder 🤍"

Why: @cocolifestyleresort's automation never replied to us, across two attempts, one of them from a follower. Their reel has 20,800 comments on it. Twenty thousand people raised their hand and nobody caught them.

What the three flows are actually testing

1 vs 2 — does a conversation with a button and an email capture beat one straight message? 1 vs 3 — does leading with why we do this beat leading with the offer? 2 vs 3 — is the button worth anything at all?

Every email captured goes to one dedicated Klaviyo list, so we can see which flow filled it fastest.

07

Before the first one goes up

Checklist

The landing page is built and waiting

Join the HOLLA Circle is created on the site with the whole offer written out and the sign-up form underneath it. It is not published yet — it goes live the moment the affiliate side is configured, and not before, so nobody signs up for something that isn't switched on. Preview it from the Shopify admin under Content → Pages.

Left for later, on purpose

  • The affiliate program itself. Deliberately not touched yet. Three things it needs — the $25 bonus, her own auto-generated code, and the automatic 10% for her friends — all sit behind an UpPromote plan upgrade, and that's a separate decision. Until then codes get issued by hand and the bonus gets paid manually. Nothing here waits on it: the videos, the ManyChat flows and the landing page can all be finished first.
  • A/B testing the offer itself. Not in these nine runs. The offer stays identical throughout, because comments per like is driven by the caption — nobody who comments has read the commission terms yet. Once we know which creative and flow win, we test the offer on the people already in the DM. Far cheaper: one reel running instead of nine.

The one rule across all three

The tool is visible and in use in the first frame. Every strong reel in the set does it and every weak one doesn't. If a cut doesn't have the product in it, it isn't the opening frame.

And nothing about the discount, the code or the money appears on screen in any of them. That all lives in the DM.
