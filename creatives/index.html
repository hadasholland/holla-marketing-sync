04

Two things that apply to all three

Ask for the follow. Never block on it.

If she doesn't follow us, put this line on top of the first message and send the message anyway:

"Quick thing, follow @hollahair.pro so my messages reach you instead of landing in your requests folder 🤍"

Test it yourself before it goes live

Comment the trigger word from a phone that doesn't follow us and check the DM actually arrives. Then check again once a week while it runs.

@cocolifestyleresort skipped this. Their reel has 20,800 comments and the automation never replies.
